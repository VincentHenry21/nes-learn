Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. NES:Learn sets no cookies

NES:Learn does not set any cookies of its own, first party or third party. Visiting the app does not place a cookie on your device from NES:Learn.

2. What is used instead: local storage

Your name and progress are stored in your browser local storage on your own device, under the key nes_learn_v1. Local storage is not a cookie: it is not sent to a server with page requests, and it stays on the device until you clear it in the app or in your browser settings.

3. Why local storage is used

Local storage is what lets the app remember your name, languages, lessons, tests, and streak between visits without any account or server.

4. If local storage is cleared or blocked

If you clear the app data in Profile, or your browser clears site data, your progress is removed from that device and cannot be recovered by NES:Learn. Exporting your data in Profile gives you a backup file you can import later.

5. No third party cookies from the app

The app loads no advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts, so it adds no third party cookies either. Your browser itself may still apply its own settings to other sites, which is outside this app control.
