Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. About this app

NES:Learn is a language learning app with the tagline Learn Beyond Language. It offers vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening and speaking practice, tests, and certification preparation across 8 languages and CEFR levels A1 to C2.

2. Free use, no account

NES:Learn is free to use. There is no account, no sign in, no subscription, and no fee. You only need to enter a name so the app can greet you, and you can change that name anytime in Profile.

3. Your data stays on your device

Your name, selected languages, lesson progress, test history, saved words, streak, and study activity are stored only in your browser on your own device. NES:Learn has no server of its own and sends none of this data anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

4. Spoken playback and real voices

When you tap Listen, the word or letter is spoken by your device speech feature where supported. Real human voice recordings are not included yet. Where a real voice recording exists for a word, the word card labels it Real voice, and otherwise it labels it Device voice. Nothing in the app claims a device voice is a human recording.

5. Certification preparation only

Certification sections prepare you for exams such as IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge English, HSK, Goethe-Zertifikat, DELF and DALF, TOPIK, JLPT, DELE, and the Myanmar structured proficiency pathway. NES:Learn does not issue certificates and has no partnership with any exam body. Passing a practice test in the app is not an official result.

6. Acceptable use

Use the app for personal learning. Do not try to break it, copy its content banks for redistribution as your own, or present practice results as official qualifications.

7. No warranty

The app is provided as it is for learning. Lesson content uses standard, well established language material, and where a pronunciation is uncertain it is left blank rather than guessed. NES:Learn does not guarantee any exam outcome.
