Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Summary

NES:Learn collects nothing on a server, because it has no server. Everything you do in the app stays in your browser on your own device.

2. What is stored on your device

The app stores one record in your browser local storage, under the key nes_learn_v1. It holds your name, selected languages, current language, preferences such as theme, daily goal, and spoken playback, and per language progress: completed lessons, exam results, test history, saved and learned words, study time, streak, and daily activity. It also stores whether you accepted the Read Before You Use The App notice.

3. What is not collected

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not ask for your email, password, location, contacts, or payment details, and it never sends your name or progress to any third party.

4. Spoken playback

When you tap Listen, the text is spoken by your device own speech feature where supported. That playback is handled by your device, not by NES:Learn, and no recording of your voice is made by the app.

5. Cookies

NES:Learn sets no cookies of its own. Your progress is kept in browser local storage, not in a cookie. See the Cookie Policy for details.

6. Your controls

In Profile you can export your data as JSON, import it back, or clear all local data. Clearing all local data removes your name, progress, and acceptance of the Read Before You Use The App notice from that device, so the notice will appear again on next use.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
